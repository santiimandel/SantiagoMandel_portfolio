    SH010Hard-surface · Fan art · Breaking Bad

    Hector's
    wheelchair

    A prop study of Hector Salamanca's chair: tubular frame, spoked wheels, the oxygen tank and the bell he rings instead of talking.

    Modeled in Blender piece by piece, unwrapped across two UDIM tiles and textured in Substance Painter, down to the glowing pressure readout on the tank. The viewport above loads the same mesh and maps you'd hand to a game or a configurator. Use MAPS to inspect each texture channel and WIRE for the quad topology.

    Eladio

    An original character, built from scratch: a cap, a full beard, a patterned shirt and red sneakers.

    Low-poly base mesh with clean loops around the mouth, eyes and fingers so it deforms well, then subdivided, textured in Substance Painter and rigged in Blender for the final animation.
